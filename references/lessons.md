# Lessons from site work

Dated, newest first. Each lesson is also written into the reference it belongs to.

## 2026-11-19
- **Moth is raw white ash until the final finish (alican).** The web GLBs (v6w, v7c) carried a near-black ash texture, so the home hero and cards showed a black case while the text said raw white ash. `scripts/glb_recolor_texture.py` swaps a material's base-colour texture for a recoloured copy that keeps the grain. Check a model's colours against the page text before publishing it.

## 2026-11-19
- **"Outdated" pages that pass the audit.** the robot project and Info passed `theme_audit.mjs` (right fonts, inks, no plates) but still looked old next to Nine Rests: centred layout, grey tracked caps subtitles, boxed cards, a ruled stats band. The audit checks the brand tokens, not the layout, so the check loop now also compares every reworked page side by side with Nine Rests (brand-on-the-web.md, "The current page look"). Pages sharing a template are restyled once through a shared stylesheet loaded last (`assets/lc-sheet.css`) rather than by patching each page's own overrides.

## 2026-11-18
- **Latest version everywhere (the owner's rule).** The Moth page moved to v6w (with the drawer stand) but the home hero still loaded v6s. `version_check.py` now flags any page using an older version of a file than its folder holds.
- **A bench can be sculpture.** Asked for "sculpture only" on the Sculpture page, I moved the Studio Shrine Bench to Furniture because it's a bench; the owner moved it straight back: carved pieces are sculpture even when usable. Don't sort by function; ask before moving a piece between sections.
- **Nav buttons jumped sides between pages (the owner: "these buttons shouldnt bounce").** The home page had work nav left, logo centre, studio nav right; every other page had logo left and all nav right. The shared header now uses the home layout and both have the same phone burger. `theme_audit.mjs` records where each nav word sits (as a % of the width) and fails any page more than 1% off the home page, on desktop and phone. A 5% vs 6% logo position on phone came from 18px vs 23px side padding.
- **Cards matched across pages.** The AI, Robotics and Sculpture pages used 4:3 tiles with three caption lines; the owner asked for them to match home. All project lists now use the home card (inline-layout.md, Project cards), and `lint_page.py` errors on the old tile styles. A skill card with a 3D model (Concrete Mold Casting) shows one K pot cut from the K mold GLB: keep the `CAST*` nodes, drop the rest, and carry the meshopt-compressed buffer views over as they are.
- **Crop on the subject.** Mandelbulb tiles were centred on the image, not on the subject (Bulb's centre of the burst is 60% across). Use `object-position` worked out from the focal point (inline-layout.md).
- **Category nav goes to a category page.** Sculpture linked to the home page; the owner expected a Sculpture page. `lint_page.py` errors on a nav item linking to `/`.
- **Site-wide theme audit.** The owner asked whether the theme had reached every page; the skill only checked one page's source, so nobody could say. A crawl of all 22 pages found: the home page's own header (off-white bar, centred logo, split nav with different labels and a Commissions link) had drifted from the shared header on every other page, and the phone menu still used the old mauve; Dancers, the app guide and Moth use the drawing red `#96281c` for step numbers, labels and links; Dancers had a pink plate behind its safety note (fixed); parts cards and the skill pages' GitHub card are boxed. Added `scripts/theme_audit.mjs` (every page, computed styles, header consistency) and made it a step before every PR (check-loop.md 3b, brand-on-the-web.md).
- **Never repeat a project on a page (the owner's rule).** The home page Sculpture group listed Moth, Fawn Orchard and Nine Rests, which were already in the hero and the Nine Rests band above it. Each project appears once per page; `lint_page.py` now errors on it.
- **Screenshots of pages with looping video.** `networkidle` never fires while a video loops, so `shoot.mjs` timed out on /k-concrete-vessels. It now waits for `load` and gives network-idle 10 s at most.
- **Inline template, drawing labels.** First test of the project-page template: an 860-unit SVG shown 460 px wide turned 15-unit labels into 8 px text, and captions were left-aligned under centred media. Fixed by sizing labels for the shown width (inline-layout.md) and one square frame per column with centred captions (template).
- **Home page plumb line (GRAVITY / GRACE).** The home page is `layout: raw`: a full HTML document of its own, with no shared header, nav or footer added by the build, so nav or font changes in `site.css` don't reach it and have to be made there too. The plumb line swings on scroll or touch and only turns firebrick, with GRAVITY and GRACE, when it comes to rest: that is the only place the red appears. Motion changes need several screenshots or a recording to check, not one still. **Polish pass (PR #3):** the flat grey cone read as clip art; a small object drawn in SVG needs a material (brass body, steel tip via horizontal linear gradients), a few turned-ring hairlines, and where it attaches (knot, pin). Labels with a hard 3px light halo over a busy photo read as outlined and blurry; use the site's on-photo convention instead: white text with a soft dark edge (two blurred dark copies, tight about 1px plus wide about 5px) on photos, plain ink with no halo on the plain cyc, 13px minimum. Check the coloured state over the brightest part of the photo too: pale red on gold vanished until its edge went deep red. In headless Chromium with SwiftShader the pendulum takes over 30 s to settle, so wait or force `.on` to capture the rest state. A hidden (`display:none`) SVG still runs its rAF loop and wrote NaN every frame on phones; skip drawing when its box is 0.
- **MouthPad photo (PR #2).** Someone else's product photo: use their largest original (1632 x 805 from the maker's site), keep it unaltered, credit and link them in a mono caption under it, give the `<img>` its real width and height, cap it at 900 px on desktop and full column on phone, and check both widths before the PR. A white-background product shot sits straight on the page with no frame.
- **Moth: viewer and drawing inline.** The 3D viewer and the dimension drawing were in separate sections, so nobody read them together and a stale viewer went unnoticed. They now sit side by side with the spec table (inline-layout.md). The same page shows the cost of stacking overrides: `.ds-stand` ended up defined six times with `!important`. Change the rule in place (site-structure.md); `lint_page.py` warns when a selector is defined three or more times.
- **Mandelbulbs: matching a fractal preset to its originals (Syd, 19 Nov 2026).** The first Syd pass stood the camera inside the box city and shipped crunchy, cluttered blocks that looked nothing like the renders. What made it read right: find what the originals are physically doing (Syd is an ultra-wide lens, 145°, hugging the outer face of the scale-3 box, so slabs stretch to a vanishing point over black void), then smooth the surface (reflection off, step 0.5, one colour band per form via `colF` 1). Judge at the real canvas size: the engine's surface epsilon scales with pixel size, so a 700 px test render showed a horizon and void that moved at 1240 px; check desktop (1440 at 1.6x) and phone before settling. Sample the palette from the original with k-means instead of guessing. The kit's score is a guide only: the old bad preset already beat the leave-one-out ceiling.
- 2026-11-19: merging GLBs with trimesh drops material extensions (KHR_materials_transmission, ior, emissive_strength). Clear acrylic then renders opaque white over anything behind it, and screens lose their glow. Copy the `extensions` block back onto each material by name from the source GLBs before compressing, and add the extension names to `extensionsUsed`.
- 2026-11-19: trimesh also writes every texture as PNG and can point two images at one buffer view. If a repack re-encodes that view as JPEG, update the mimeType on every image that shares it, or gltf-transform's texture step fails with "width 0".
- 2026-11-19: `glb_flicker_check.mjs` uses the UV u-value as "colour" and a bounding-box overlap, so a flat textured face (a silkscreened plate, a logo) split into triangles shows hundreds of false hits. Confirm a hit by intersecting the actual coplanar triangles (shapely union area against the sum of areas) before changing the model.
- **2026-11-19 Dancers page stale after a build audit.** All 24 machines were re-rendered and re-audited after the page's last build, but `version_check.py` only compares files inside the repo, so it passed. The page kept old renders, prices, guides and a Drag Queen solenoid diagram of a design that no longer existed; the page builder also refreshed images and buy lists but never the guides, recipes or how-it-works text, and closed one section early. Added `scripts/source_check.py` and the repo's `tools/sources.json` (page to project-file globs); it flags any source newer than the page's last commit. When rebuilding a section, regenerate every block from its source and check the HTML nests (one `<section>` open per close).
